# Privacy Policy — ClipWeld Lite

**Publisher:** ThorTech Softwares  
**Application:** ClipWeld Lite for Windows  
**Last updated:** 9 November 2026

## Overview

ClipWeld Lite is a free, offline video clipping and editing application. Its video and audio analysis, editing, project storage, and MP4 exports are designed to run locally on your Windows computer.

## Information and files

ClipWeld Lite does not require an account and does not include in-app analytics, advertising trackers, or feedback submission forms. The application does not automatically upload your videos, audio, projects, or exports to ThorTech Softwares or a cloud processing service.

Videos and audio you choose to import may contain personal information, but they are processed locally on your device. Projects, temporary processing files, and exported media remain on your device, subject to your chosen file locations and Windows settings.

## Internet access and third parties

Core editing functions do not require an internet connection. The application uses bundled components, including FFmpeg, for local media processing. Installing and updating the application through Microsoft Store is governed by Microsoft's own terms and privacy practices. If you choose to store or back up files in a cloud-synced folder, the provider of that folder may process those files under its own privacy policy.

## Data retention and control

ThorTech Softwares does not receive your locally processed media or projects through ClipWeld Lite. You control the local files and can delete them using Windows. Uninstalling the application may not delete projects or exports saved outside its application data folders.

## Earlier beta feedback

Earlier development builds included a feedback submission feature. It has been removed from ClipWeld Lite v0.4.2. This statement does not claim that historical feedback records from earlier builds have already been deleted; any such records require separate retention and deletion review.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be available on the public privacy policy page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact ThorTech Softwares through the support contact published on the application's Microsoft Store listing.
